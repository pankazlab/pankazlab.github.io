Biology, read as data

We, The Data Biology Group, are a team of interdisciplinary researchers that combines computational modeling, biological data science and experimental biology to understand complex biological systems and disease mechanisms.

Explore our researchJoin the group

What is Data Biology?

Data Biology is an advanced scientific discipline that investigates biology through the generation, analysis, integration and interpretation of biological data. It goes beyond bioinformatics or simply analyzing existing datasets. It connects experimental biology, computational biology, data science, and theoretical biology to develop a more quantitative and integrated understanding of living systems and disease.

Research

Progression of Parkinson’s Disease

We investigate molecular, cellular, and clinical changes associated with Parkinson’s disease progression, with emphasis on non-motor symptoms, circulating biomarkers, and systemic manifestations. Experimental disease models and patient-derived data are integrated to understand disease-associated mechanisms.

Cardiac Pathology

We study cardiac dysfunction associated with Parkinson’s disease and other cardiovascular conditions, focusing on cardiomyocyte function, molecular changes, and neurocardiac communication. Experimental models and multi-omics approaches are used to identify mechanisms linking neural and cardiac pathology.

AI/ML for Discovery

We develop and apply AI/ML approaches to discover biomarkers, disease-associated patterns, and predictive signatures from clinical, molecular, imaging, and multi-omics data. The goal is to move beyond prediction toward biologically interpretable insights.

Theoretical and Computational Models

We develop mathematical, network, and computational models to represent biological relationships and generate mechanistic hypotheses. These models help connect molecular and cellular observations with system-level behavior and guide experimental investigation.
